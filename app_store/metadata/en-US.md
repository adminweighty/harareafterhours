## Description

Harare comes alive after dark.

Step into Tari's story and build a name across a city of deliveries, stolen goods, street pursuits, dangerous rivals and choices that follow you home.

Your first night begins with a wrong delivery. Meet Rudo, recover Chipo's stolen satchel through the market or service route, call on Tino for support and return to the city in free roam. From there, follow a 30-mission campaign route through six connected districts as Tari earns trust, helps the community and works toward the biggest night yet.

EXPLORE THE CITY

Walk through illuminated evening streets, enter key locations and take the wheel when a mission calls for speed. Streetlights, traffic, pedestrians and a star-filled sky give every route its own atmosphere.

CHASE, DRIVE AND FIGHT

Track clearly marked threats, pursue thieves on foot, fight at close range or engage enemies from a distance. Use pistols, rifles and melee equipment, then move behind cover and manage your health and ammunition.

MAKE THE STORY YOURS

Choose routes, make mission decisions and build crew trust or community support. Checkpoints preserve your progress, while completed missions lead back into free roam.

PLAY YOUR WAY

• 30 campaign missions across six districts
• Beginner, Intermediate and Expert difficulty levels
• Full-character and closer camera views
• Walking, sprinting, combat and driveable vehicles
• Customizable weapons, clothing, eyewear and headwear
• Adjustable controls, aiming sensitivity and graphics
• Sound controls and a shuffleable soundtrack
• Optional player identity and online score rankings
• Offline story progression with saved checkpoints

Campaign missions never require a purchase, a watched advertisement or a paid retry. Learn the streets, protect the people who trust you and decide what kind of name Tari will leave behind.

Harare Nights is a fictional action-adventure story inspired by the energy of urban Harare.

## App Review notes

No account is required. The game opens directly into the evening city.

Tap **Menu** in the upper-right corner to access difficulty, controls, sound, graphics, character customization and rankings. The leaderboard accepts a player name; email is optional. A selected character photo remains on the device and is not uploaded with campaign progress.

To review the opening mission, follow the gold marker to Rudo and tap **TALK**. Follow the marked satchel runner, recover the satchel directly or ask Tino to block the exit, then return it to Chipo. The player continues in free roam after completion.

The game contains fictional combat, blood effects and criminal characters. Complete the App Store age-rating questionnaire according to the final release build's actual frequency and intensity.
